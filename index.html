Star Schema Data Model Architecture

The model contains 4 Dimension tables and 3 Fact tables configured in a Star Schema:

  • Dim_Department (1) to (N) Dim_Employee [DepartmentID]
  • Dim_JobRole (1) to (N) Dim_Employee [JobRoleID]
  • Dim_Employee (1) to (N) Fact_Payroll [EmployeeID]
  • Dim_Employee (1) to (N) Fact_Performance [EmployeeID]
  • Dim_Employee (1) to (N) Fact_Attrition [EmployeeID]
  • Dim_Date (1) to (N) Fact tables [Active PayDate, ReviewDate, ExitDate]
